# Presenter notes - Leader session 2: OKRs vs KPIs vs metrics

Audience: C-level and upper management. The vocabulary session. Dry on paper, so keep it moving
with the metric tree, which is the visual everyone remembers.

## Before the session
- Collect two or three real goals from the leadership team beforehand and be ready to classify
  them live as OKR, KPI, or guardrail. Live classification of their own goals is the whole value.
- Be ready for the Google 0.7 question with a caveat: it is a Google norm to adapt, not a law.
  Say that out loud or someone will import it as gospel.
- Sketch the Cartwheel tree (Revenue = Sessions x Conversion x AOV) on the board before people
  arrive if you have a whiteboard. Returning to a drawing beats re-summoning a slide.

## Timing (45 min)
- 0-3: Recap of session 1, the three muddled words
- 3-20: Part 1 - OKR anatomy, committed vs stretch, the 0-1 score
- 20-33: Part 2 - change vs health, and the metric tree
- 33-40: Walk-through 1 - is it an OKR, a KPI, or a guardrail?
- 40-43: Walk-through 2 - give every Cartwheel team a leaf to own
- 43-45: Q&A

## Walk-through run-of-show
- Walk-through 1: run the four Cartwheel numbers (checkout conversion, gross margin, repeat
  purchase, uptime) as a fast show-of-hands vote before revealing. Disagreement is the teaching
  moment: the same number can be an OKR this quarter and a guardrail next.
- Land the sentence out loud: "This quarter we change conversion, while we hold margin and
  uptime." Leaders repeat sentences, not frameworks.
- Walk-through 2: the beat that matters is refusing to grade anyone on Revenue. Say plainly that
  four teams move it, so it is watched together and graded to nobody.

## Watch for
- "So a KPI is just an OKR we did not set a target on." Not quite: change versus steady-state
  health is the distinction, not whether a number exists.
- Someone wanting fifteen key results. Fewer and bolder. Three is plenty.
- The stretch-goal objection: "if we do not expect 1.0, people will sandbag." Answer with the
  decoupling rule - sandbagging is what happens when the score sets pay.
- Confusion between a guardrail and a key result. A guardrail is watched and honoured; a key
  result is graded.

## Cuts if long
- The 0-1 scoring mechanics can compress: state the scale, mark it as self-study detail.
- Walk-through 2 can be homework if the tree discussion in Part 2 already assigned owners.
- Never cut the change-versus-health distinction. It is the reason the session exists, and a3
  and b4 both build directly on the tree.
