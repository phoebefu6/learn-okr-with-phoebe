# Presenter notes - Leader session 6: Rollout, cadence, and culture

Audience: C-level and upper management. Capstone of the leader track. The job here is to convert
six sessions of design into a rollout the room will actually authorise.

## Before the session
- Know where your organisation genuinely is on the crawl-walk-run path. If you present run and
  the room is at crawl, you lose them.
- Have Grove's task-relevant maturity ready as a concrete example, not a phrase - a seasoned
  person on a brand-new task needs MORE oversight, not less. That inversion is the memorable beat.
- Prepare the Adobe, Deloitte and Gallup references as reported findings, and be honest that the
  headline numbers are as-reported, not independently verified here.
- Have a real answer to "when do we start?" A capstone that ends without a first step reads as a
  seminar.

## Timing (45 min)
- 0-3: Recap of the whole track, the last mile of a fair system
- 3-18: Part 1 - cadence, task-relevant maturity, crawl-walk-run rollout
- 18-30: Part 2 - culture, psychological safety, the guardrails
- 30-38: Walk-through 1 - match cadence to maturity, not to rank
- 38-43: Walk-through 2 - when NOT to measure
- 43-45: Wrap, and the pointer into the manager track

## Walk-through run-of-show
- Walk-through 1: three people, one current task each, rate maturity, set frequency from maturity.
  The trap to surface deliberately: giving the most senior person the least oversight on a task
  that is new to them. Let someone in the room catch it.
- Walk-through 2 is the one leaders remember, because it gives permission to stop. Three tests:
  will it be gamed, is it uncontrollable, will anyone act on it? Any yes to the first two or no to
  the third means do not measure it, or watch it as context only.
- Close by naming the manager track: the leader designs the system, b1 to b10 runs it. If both
  tracks are in the room, this is the handoff.

## Watch for
- Psychological safety being heard as softness. It is the condition for honest reporting; without
  it your steering data is polite fiction.
- Cadence being set by rank because that is the existing calendar. Repeat: maturity, not title.
- Rollout ambition. A room that has enjoyed six sessions will want to launch everywhere at once.
  Crawl-walk-run, and a first quarter with no consequences attached.
- Anyone leaving without the decoupling rule intact. Restate it in the wrap.

## Cuts if long
- The crawl-walk-run detail compresses to naming the three stages.
- Walk-through 1 can run on one person instead of three.
- Never cut Walk-through 2 or the wrap. Permission not to measure is the leader-track payoff, and
  the wrap is where the room commits to a first step.
